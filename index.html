FAQ

Course schema QA constraints are explicit.

Does it crawl course pages?

No. It produces a launch QA brief from pasted metadata and notes only.

Does it call Search Console or Rich Results Test?

No. This MVP is browser-local and does not call Search Console, Rich Results Test, LMS platforms, or external APIs.

Does it connect to an LMS?

No. Provider, catalog, and course launch details are pasted as notes for static QA only.

Does it guarantee eligibility or rankings?

No. It is technical SEO and launch QA guidance, not education compliance, accreditation, eligibility, ranking, indexing, enrollment operations, or student-support advice.
